Soulful vocals, rock edges, pop instincts.

Kyla Charter is a Toronto singer-songwriter, vocalist and rhythm guitar player. Her own description of her sound: soulful vocals with rock tonalities and pop sensibilities.

Her debut album, Edible Flowers, was released on April 8, 2022. She has also sung background vocals for July Talk, Alessia Cara, Zaki Ibrahim, Rich Aucoin and Patrick Watson.
