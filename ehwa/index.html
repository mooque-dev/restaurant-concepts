Our story

A hanbok,
a smartphone,
a soju bar.

Our host wears a hanbok and carries a smartphone. The menu works the same way: old-school Korean diner classics like tonkatsu and hamburg steak next to mala rosé tteokbokki and four-flavour chicken.

Uptown or downtown, the soju is cold and the kitchen is open late.
