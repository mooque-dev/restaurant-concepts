How it works

Free to look. Simple to launch.

No contracts to start. If you like the concept, we put it live on your domain.

1Free concept

What we look at first.

The questions diners ask in the first five seconds on your site. Most restaurant sites miss at least four.

01Are you open right now?
02Can I book or call in one tap?
03Is the menu a page, not a PDF?
04Does the first screen show food?
05Is the site secure (HTTPS)?
06Does it load fast on a phone?
07Do hours match Google Maps?
08Are reviews recent and real?
09Is every location covered?
10Can your community read it?
