How to eat jokbal

Wrap it,
dip it, one bite.

First time? Here's how it's eaten in Korea.

01Start with lettuce

Lay a lettuce or perilla leaf flat in your palm.

02Add meat and sides

A slice of jokbal or bossam, then a little kimchi, garlic and sauce.

03Wrap and eat

Fold it up and eat it in one bite. Bossam literally means wrapped.
